Controls

  • Drag empty space to pan; wheel or pinch to zoom; F fit
  • Click a polygon to select (innermost wins)
  • Drag a white handle to move a vertex
  • Double-click a handle to delete it, an edge to add one
  • Del delete label · A accept prediction as label
  • D draw: click points, click the first point or press Enter to close, Esc cancel
  • 1–7 set category of selected label
  • N/P next/prev · R reviewed · Z undo · T toggle predictions · H hide/show all outlines

Saving

Edits save in this browser automatically. Opened from the lab server, they also upload to the PC (badge at top right); offline edits upload when the connection returns. Opened from a zip, Export regularly and Import the JSON into the online page later.
