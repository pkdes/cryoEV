Controls

  • Drag empty space to pan; wheel or pinch to zoom; F fit
  • Click a polygon to select (innermost wins)
  • Drag a white handle to move a vertex
  • Click the outline to add a point; right-click a point (phone: double-tap) to delete it
  • Several points: click one, shift-click more, or Shift-drag a box; Del deletes them (at least 3 stay), Esc clears
  • Selected prediction: A accept (as its predicted class) · X reject (not an object). Moving, adding or deleting one of its vertices accepts it too (Undo in the message, or Z)
  • Del delete label
  • D freehand: drag around the object · V vertex: tap points, tap the first point or Enter to close · Esc cancel. In both, right/middle-drag or two fingers pan
  • E redraw the selected outline (keeps class and id)
  • 1–8 or a class chip: reclass the selection; also the class for new shapes
  • L Low conf tag on the selection, on / off (still counted; a selected unsure outline is accepted as low conf). Its note and the image's reject / note are in this panel
  • Image reject and note: Z doesn't undo them. Change them in their dialog (the Not rejected chip un-rejects)
  • N/P next/prev · R reviewed · Z undo · T toggle predictions · H hide/show all outlines
